FishFlow: Pixel Ecosystem Sandbox

Welcome to FishFlow, an interactive cellular automata physics sandbox where you can build and destroy a living, breathing ecosystem pixel by pixel.

How to Play

  • Water & Sand: Build the foundation of your tank.
  • Smart Seeds & Plants: Drop seeds to grow procedural canopies.
  • Fish & Mites: Add AI-driven creatures that hunt for food.
  • Hazardous Gas & Fire: Release flammable gas and ignite it for explosive chain reactions!
